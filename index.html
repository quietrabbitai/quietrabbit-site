Your life has natural boundaries.
Your AI should too.

Your personal AI. Built to grow, always yours.

Quiet Rabbit is a self-hosted AI that organizes itself around the different parts of your life — keeping them separate, keeping them private, and getting better at helping you over time. Runs on your own computer. No subscriptions. No data sharing. No surprises.

Why this exists

Two things that actually happened, and probably sound familiar.

When your AI knows too much

After helping with a computer problem, an AI casually referenced an upcoming mother-daughter trip and offered to build a reading list for it. Nobody asked. Nobody wanted that. That's not helpfulness — that's your private life being used as data.

When your AI knows too little

Business writing, college planning, yard sign design, and tech support all crammed into one place with no separation. Every conversation starts from scratch. Your AI should know what matters in each part of your life — without mixing everything together.

Quiet Rabbit solves both. Your work life doesn't bleed into your personal life. Your medical history never touches your finances. Each part of your life gets its own AI context — with its own memory, its own knowledge of you, and nothing crossing over without your explicit permission.

How we think about this

Your life already has natural boundaries. Your AI should respect them.

You don't talk to your doctor the way you talk to your boss. You don't bring your tax questions to the family dinner table. You call your doctor about your health, your accountant about your taxes, your tech-savvy friend about your laptop. That's not secrecy — that's just how a well-organized life works.

We didn't invent that idea. We just built an AI that finally respects it.

In Quiet Rabbit, each part of your life — your work life, your home life, your personal life — is its own separate world. Within each one, you can go as deep as you want: a cooking focus, a job search, a home renovation, a health question. Every pursuit is tracked, remembered, and built on over time. What happens in one world stays there. What you learn in one compounds into something genuinely useful — for you, not for anyone else.

And unlike every AI tool you've used before, Quiet Rabbit runs on your own computer. The context it builds about you never leaves your machine without you saying so.

How it works

Organized the way your life actually works.

You don't have to learn anything new to get started. Quiet Rabbit figures out what you need and guides you through everything in plain conversation.

01

Separate lives for separate parts of life

Your work life, your personal life, your home life — each one lives in its own world. What you share in one never appears in another. That's not a setting you configure. That's how the system is built, at every level, all the way down.

02

Pick up exactly where you left off

Looking for a new car? Planning a bathroom renovation? Searching for a new job? Each pursuit lives in its own named topic — complete with everything you've researched, decided, and saved. Come back a week later and it's right where you left it, with full context intact.

03

It knows you — in each part of your life

Quiet Rabbit learns what's relevant to each area of your life and brings it to the conversation automatically. Your writing style, your preferences, your constraints — all there, all private, never shared between areas unless you say so.

04

Runs on your computer, not someone else's

A guided setup gets everything running on your own hardware. Your data stays where you put it. No company reading your conversations to train their next model. For sensitive topics — health, legal, finances — nothing ever leaves your machine at all.

05

The right help for the right task, automatically

Writing a cover letter is different from researching a product purchase. Cooking a new dish is different from planning a trip. Quiet Rabbit figures out the right kind of assistance for what you're actually doing — and routes it through the right tools behind the scenes, without you having to think about it.

Simple to start. Powerful if you want it.

A first-time user gets a guided conversation that sets everything up — no configuration files, no technical decisions required. Just talk to it. Quiet Rabbit figures out the rest.

And when you're ready to go deeper — custom Focuses, connected tools, shared workflows — it's all there. The same system that works beautifully for a non-technical user scales to whatever you want to build with it.

Always improving

It gets smarter the more you use it. And always asks before changing anything.

Quiet Rabbit isn't static. Every conversation, every saved document, every piece of feedback makes it more accurate and more personal — but only in the ways you approve.

The gap widens over time

The context Quiet Rabbit builds about you is yours — and it compounds. The longer you use it, the more accurately it understands your preferences, your voice, your patterns. That gap between QR and a generic AI tool grows every session. It can't be replicated by anyone who doesn't have your history.

It picks up on how you communicate

You don't write a cover letter the way you'd text a friend. You don't email your manager the way you talk to your doctor. Quiet Rabbit picks up on those differences — in each part of your life separately — so the help it gives always sounds like it belongs there.

Nothing changes without your say-so

Quiet Rabbit can suggest improvements to how it works — better routing, smarter defaults, updated preferences. But it never applies them automatically. You review every suggestion and decide what happens to your setup. Always. No silent updates, no hidden changes.

Tells you plainly when something's off

When something isn't working as well as it should, Quiet Rabbit tells you what's happening and what you can do about it — in plain language, not error codes. No silent failures, no guessing about what went wrong.

Privacy commitments

Not fine print. The actual product.

Privacy isn't a setting you turn on. It's how Quiet Rabbit is built, from the ground up. These aren't aspirations — they're constraints we've designed around.

  • Your data never leaves your device without you saying so Local AI runs directly on your hardware. No conversation is sent anywhere by default. You choose if and when anything leaves your machine — and Quiet Rabbit tells you exactly what's going where before it happens.
  • Sensitive topics stay fully local. Always. Health, legal, and financial conversations never touch an external server — not even to check something. These areas are hardwired to run on your device only. That's not a default you can accidentally change.
  • No telemetry. What you do with it is your business. Quiet Rabbit collects no usage data, no analytics, no crash reports sent anywhere. What happens on your machine stays on your machine. Full stop.
  • Every external action asks first When a task would send anything outside your machine, Quiet Rabbit tells you in plain language exactly what's going where — before it happens, not after. You can always say no.
  • Nothing changes without your approval Quiet Rabbit can suggest improvements to how it works, but it never applies them automatically. You review every suggestion and decide what happens to your setup.

Being built carefully. Worth the wait.

Quiet Rabbit isn't ready for public use yet. It's being built deliberately — tested on real life before it's handed to anyone else. Watch the repository for updates, or read the code if you want to understand what's being built and why.
