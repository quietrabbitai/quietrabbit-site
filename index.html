Your life has natural boundaries.
Your AI should too.

"Your personal AI. Simple to start, built to grow, always yours."

Quiet Rabbit is a self-hosted AI that keeps every part of your life where it belongs: organized, private, and completely under your control. No subscriptions. No data sharing. Runs on your own computer.

Why this exists

Two things that actually happened, and probably sound familiar.

When your AI knows too much

After helping with a computer problem, an AI casually referenced an upcoming mother-daughter trip and offered to build a reading list for it. Nobody asked. Nobody wanted that. That's not helpfulness. That's your private life being used as data.

When your AI knows too little

Business writing, college planning, yard sign design, and tech support all crammed into one place with no separation. Every conversation starts from scratch. Your AI should know what matters in each part of your life, without mixing everything together.

Quiet Rabbit solves both. Your work life doesn't bleed into your personal life. Your medical history never touches your finances. Each part of your life gets its own AI, with its own context, its own memory, and nothing crossing over without your permission.

How we think about this

Your life already has natural boundaries. Your AI should respect them.

You don't talk to your doctor the way you talk to your boss. You don't bring your tax questions to the family dinner table. You call your doctor about your health, your accountant about your taxes, your gardener about the weeds taking over the back bed, your tech-savvy friend about your laptop. That's not secrecy. That's just how a well-organized life works.

We didn't invent that idea. We just built an AI that finally respects it.

Every part of your life gets its own space in Quiet Rabbit: its own context, its own memory, its own way of helping you. Your health space has never heard of your quarterly report. Your work space doesn't know about your weekend plans. What happens in one, stays in one.

And when you have a question, you get the right kind of help for it. We're building that instinct into every part of the system.

How it works

Organized the way your life actually works.

You don't have to learn anything new to get started. Quiet Rabbit figures out what you need and guides you through everything in plain conversation.

01

Separate spaces for separate parts of life

Work, health, family, finances: each has its own space. What you share in one never appears in another. That's not a setting — that's how it's built.

02

It remembers what matters, in each space

You shouldn't have to re-explain yourself every time. Quiet Rabbit learns what's relevant to each part of your life and brings it to the conversation automatically, without mixing things together.

03

Runs on your computer, not someone else's

A guided setup gets everything running on your hardware. Your data stays where you put it. No company reading your conversations to train their next model.

04

Plain conversation, not prompts

You don't need to know how to write a prompt or what a model is. Just talk to it. Quiet Rabbit figures out the right way to help and assembles the right kind of assistance behind the scenes.

05

The right horsepower for the right task

Not every question needs the same muscle behind it. Simple tasks run quickly and quietly on your device. For anything that would benefit from more horsepower, Quiet Rabbit can optionally go further, but only when you say so, and only after it tells you exactly what's going where.

Simple to start. Built to grow.

First-time users get a guided conversation that sets everything up. No configuration files, no technical decisions. And when you're ready to go deeper, advanced settings, custom automations, and your own tools are all there waiting.

Quiet Rabbit also picks the right tool for the job. A simple question gets a simple answer, not a semi truck sent to pick up groceries.

Always improving

It gets better the more you use it. And always asks before changing anything.

Quiet Rabbit isn't static. It pays attention, surfaces suggestions, and adapts, but only with your approval. Nothing changes on its own.

It picks up on how you communicate, not just what you need

You don't write a cover letter the way you'd text a friend. You don't email your manager the way you talk to your kids. Quiet Rabbit picks up on those differences, in each space separately, so the help it gives always sounds like it belongs in that part of your life.

Learns how you work

It picks up on how you like things handled: what you tend to need, where you want more detail, where you want less. Not because it's building a profile. Because it's paying attention to the right things, in the right space.

Tells you when something's off

When something isn't working as well as it should, Quiet Rabbit tells you plainly what's happening and what you can do about it. No silent failures, no guessing.

You're always in control

Quiet Rabbit can suggest improvements to how it works, but it never applies them automatically. You review every suggestion and decide what happens to your setup. Always.

Privacy commitments

Not fine print. The actual product.

Privacy isn't a setting you turn on. It's how Quiet Rabbit is built, from the ground up. These aren't aspirations. They're constraints we've built around.

  • Your data never leaves your device without you saying so Local AI runs directly on your hardware. No conversation is sent anywhere by default. You choose if and when anything leaves your machine.
  • Sensitive topics stay fully local. Always. Health, legal, and financial conversations never touch an external server, not even to check something. These spaces are hardwired to run on your device only.
  • No telemetry. What you do with it is your business. Quiet Rabbit collects no usage data, no analytics, no crash reports sent home. What happens on your machine stays on your machine.
  • Every external action asks first When a task would share anything outside your machine, Quiet Rabbit tells you in plain language exactly what's going where, before it happens, not after.
  • Nothing changes without your approval Quiet Rabbit can suggest improvements to how it works, but it never applies them automatically. You review every suggestion and decide what happens to your setup.
